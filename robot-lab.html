        For grown-ups

        Each station follows the Robots chapter of the ICT book, pages 8 to 15. Tap 🔊 on any question to have it read aloud. Progress is saved in this browser only.

        Part 1: What is a robot? (pages 8–11)

        1. Robot or Not? (pages 8–9): a robot has a computer brain, can move, and has sensors. He checks a robot vacuum, a laptop, a teddy, a Mars rover, a factory arm and a puppy. The laptop is the comic's point: a computer has a brain but can't walk. The puppy shows the book's “big difference”: a robot doesn't think by itself.
        2. Build Bolt (page 9): he fits the computer brain, sensors, motor and wheels, and battery. Each part is compared to his body: brain, eyes/ears/skin, legs, and food for energy.
        3. Checkpoint 1 (pages 10–11): questions 1a–1e word for word, then the parts-to-jobs matching from question 2.
        4. Air Patrol (page 11, EcoRoute): a drone with air sensors checks smoke, dust and the AQI over a park, a busy road, a factory and a hill fort.

        Part 2: Robots at work (pages 12–14)

        1. Robot Jobs (pages 12–13, and the TechTrek matching game on page 14): he sorts robots into farm, hospital, fun, rescue, factory and space.
        2. Robot Doctor (page 14, question 3): why did the robot vacuum stop? A flat battery, a sock in the wheel, a dusty sensor, or no instruction yet.
        3. Robots or People? (page 13, Checkpoint 2 question 1, and RealRoute on page 14): which jobs suit robots and which suit people, and why space robots matter.
        4. My School Robot (page 14, question 2, and Wonder Junction): he picks a school problem and designs a robot to fix it. There are no wrong answers. His robot appears on the map afterwards.

        Part 3: Robots follow instructions (page 15)

        1. Step by Step: a program is like a recipe. He puts making toast and getting ready for school in order, then the five steps from the book's robot diagram.
        2. Code Bolt (Commands and sequencing, and TrailTreasure): he gives arrow commands to steer Bolt round the classroom, then fixes a program with a bug.

        Checkpoint 1 answers

        1a smart machine · 1b instructions · 1c computer · 1d help humans · 1e humans. Question 2: computer brain → follows instructions; sensors → acts like eyes or ears; battery → gives power; wheels → helps robot move.
