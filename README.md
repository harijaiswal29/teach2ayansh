# teach2ayansh

Learning games for Ayansh.

## Times Quest (multiplication, age 7)

Open `index.html` in any browser (phone, tablet or laptop). Progress is saved in that browser.

Five stops, from hands-on to quick recall:

1. **Laddoo Boxes**: fill equal groups and count them. This shows what "×" means.
2. **Froggy Hops**: skip counting on a number line (repeated addition).
3. **Sunflower Garden**: rows × columns. Turning the garden shows 3 × 5 = 5 × 3.
4. **Story Bazaar**: everyday word problems (auto wheels, cricket overs, idli plates).
5. **Rocket Launch**: 10 quick facts from the 2, 5, 10, 3 and 4 tables, with picture hints.

Each stop gives up to 3 stars, and a treasure chart fills in as facts are learned.
There's a "For grown-ups" section at the bottom of the map with tips and offline activities.

## Multiplication Fair (Chapter 9 of the school maths book)

Open `chapter9-fair.html`. It follows the chapter page by page, set at the school fair:

1. **Bakery Trays** (pp. 28–29): spot equal and unequal groups, fix them, then use repeated addition.
2. **Block Towers** (pp. 29–30): towers of 2, 5 and 10; the record table fills in as he guesses each total.
3. **Toy Train** (pp. 31, 35): build the sentence groups × in each group = product.
4. **Lantern Lights** (pp. 32–36): light the tables of 2, 5 and 10, find the patterns, use the "put a zero" trick for tens.
5. **Prize Booth** (pp. 36–37): checkpoint matching, drawing beads, and filling a table row.
