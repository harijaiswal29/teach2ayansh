# The Rainbow: Rainbow Bridge

English Chapter 1 "The Rainbow", book pages 7–32. Open [`rainbow-bridge.html`](rainbow-bridge.html). Page-by-page notes and answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

The poem is "The Rainbow" by Christina Rossetti. Every new idea starts with a short 💡 card, teaching is read aloud by default, and the last round of each stop is an "on your own" round with clues only after the second try.

- **Part 1, The poem** (stops 1–4, pp. 7–10): Where Do Clouds Go?, Read the Poem, What the Poem Means, Rhyme Time.
- **Part 2, Out and about** (stops 5–6, pp. 11–13): Nature Detective Walk, Trash to Treasure Trail.
- **Part 3, Nouns** (stops 7–12, pp. 13–27): Common and Proper Nouns, Plural Patrol, Snack Tray Basket and Zoo, Count It or Not?, Five-Senses Nouns, Collective Nouns (with the book's word search).
- **Part 4, Sounds, story and writing** (stops 13–16, pp. 27–32): Trigraph Tongue Twisters, Double Letter Twins, Max's Amazing Race (retold in our own words), If I Could Touch a Rainbow.
- **Grand Review** (stop 17): mixed questions from the whole chapter, with a Word Wizard certificate.
