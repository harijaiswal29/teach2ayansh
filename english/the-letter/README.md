# The Letter: Snail Mail Trail

English Chapter 4 "The Letter" (from *Frog and Toad Are Friends* by Arnold Lobel), book pages 34–54. Open [`snail-mail-trail.html`](snail-mail-trail.html). Page-by-page notes and answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Speedy the snail (the slow postman from the story) guides Ayansh along the trail. Read-aloud is on by default, story sentences can be tapped to hear them, and blue words show their meaning. The last round of every stop is an "on your own" round: the story is folded away and clues come only after a second try.

- **Part 1, Read the story** (stops 1–5, pp. 34–38): Under the Cover, Toad's Sad Time, Frog's Big Idea, Waiting at the Window, Four Days Later. Words: porch, sighed, mailbox, envelope, nap, silly, pleased.
- **Part 2, Think about the story** (stops 6–8, pp. 39–41): Who Feels This Way?, How Long Was the Wait?, Pigeon to Phone.
- **Part 3, Word power** (stops 9–14, pp. 42–48): Common or Proper? (the book's word search), This That These Those, Where Is It? (position words), How Does It Look? (adjectives of quality), How Many? How Much? (adjectives of quantity), Guess the Close-up.
- **Part 4, Nature, speaking and writing** (stops 15–17, pp. 49–52): The Eco Map, My Friendship Podcast, Write a Letter.
- **Part 5, Reading on my own** (stop 18, pp. 53–54): The Whispering Forest.
- **Grand Review** (stop 19): 10 mixed questions, two from each part, with a certificate.

Page 55 (a Tom Sawyer mood page) is a separate class-reader page and is not in the game.
