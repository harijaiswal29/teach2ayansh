The quest

    Treasure chart

    Every fact you get right fills a square. Learn 3 × 4 and you get 4 × 3 free, because turning a group around never changes the total!

    For grown-ups

    How the quest teaches

    1. Laddoo Boxes: fill equal groups with your hands, then count. This is what “×” really means.
    2. Froggy Hops: the same idea as repeated addition and skip counting on a number line. Later rounds ask him to guess before hopping.
    3. Sunflower Garden: arrays (rows × columns). Turning the garden shows that 3 × 5 = 5 × 3.
    4. Story Bazaar: everyday word problems. He first picks which sum tells the story, then solves it.
    5. Rocket Launch: quick recall of the 2, 5 and 10 tables, then 3 and 4. A wrong answer shows a picture hint, never a penalty timer.

    Ten to fifteen minutes a day works better than one long session. Let him replay a stop to win 3 stars; repeating is how facts stick. The 🔊 button reads each question aloud if reading is still new.

    Try it away from the screen

    • Dinner: put 2 rotis on each of 3 plates. “How many plates? How many on each? How many altogether?”
    • Shoes by the door: count in pairs, “2, 4, 6, 8…”, then say “4 pairs of 2 is 8.”
    • Egg tray or chocolate bar: count rows and columns, then turn it around.
    • On the road: “Each auto has 3 wheels. How many wheels on those 2 autos?”
