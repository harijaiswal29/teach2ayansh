# Rural and Urban Communities: Village–City Express

EVS Chapter 7 "Rural and Urban Communities", book pages 7–28. Open [`village-city-express.html`](village-city-express.html). Page-by-page notes and answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Ayansh rides Chhuk-Chhuk the goods train from a village to a city and back, in the book's order. Every stop teaches first with a 💡 card and an everyday example (read aloud by default), then practises; the last round of each stop is "on your own", with clues only after the second try. A 📒 revision-notes card on the map lists the key terms and common test questions with model answers.

- **Part 1, What is a community?** (stops 1–2, pp. 7–9): All Aboard!, What Is a Community?
- **Part 2, The city** (stops 3–4, pp. 9–11): The City (urban, metropolitan), Everyone Has a Job (specialised jobs).
- **Part 3, The village** (stops 5–9, pp. 11–17): The Land Changes, The Village (rural, farming, market day), Challenges in the Village (PHC, ASHA worker, rain, water), Dharnai's Solar Story, Checkpoint 1.
- **Part 4, Food on the move** (stops 10–13, pp. 18–23): The Train Loads Up, A Day in Kabir's City, City Life and Challenges, Checkpoint 2 (R or U).
- **Part 5, All connected** (stops 14–17, pp. 24–28): The Invisible Thread (interdependence), Many Different Communities, Nalanda, The Last Stop (Checkpoint 3).
- **Part 6, Exam practice** (stops 18–22): Fill in the Blanks, True or False (with correcting the false sentence), Match the Following, One-Word Answers (with spelling), Write the Answer (model short answers, then he builds them).
- **Grand Review** (stop 23): 10 mixed questions, new each time, with a Chapter 7 Community Champion certificate that shows which part to revisit.
