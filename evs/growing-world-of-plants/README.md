# The Growing World of Plants: Little Seed's Journey

EVS Chapter 4 "The Growing World of Plants", book pages 7–22. Open [`little-seeds-journey.html`](little-seeds-journey.html). Page-by-page notes, key words and the answers to every checkpoint are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Ankur the sprout guides Ayansh from a tiny seed to a big tree. Because EVS was hard last term, every fact starts with a "💡" card that explains it with an everyday example (soaking moong, a straw, a raincoat, a sweater), read aloud by default, before any question. Practice then uses the question types of a school test: fill in the blanks, true or false with the corrected sentence, match the following, one-word answers, arrange in order, label the plant, spell the key words, and short answers built word by word. The last round of every stop is an "on your own" round: clues come only after a second try. A "My plant words" card lights up each key word with its meaning, ready for revision.

- **Part 1, Seed to plant** (stops 1–4, pp. 7–9): Was the Tree Always Big?, The Life Cycle (germination as a little experiment, the five stages, the life-cycle circle), Not Only Seeds (spores, underground stem), Checkpoint 1.
- **Part 2, Plant parts and their jobs** (stops 5–10, pp. 10–14): Sprout Chaat, Plant Parts and Their Jobs (tap a plant picture; photosynthesis), Plants on My Plate, Special Trees (Bodhi tree, zero-waste coconut tree), Water Travels Up (the coloured-water experiment), Checkpoint 2.
- **Part 3, Plant homes and adaptations** (stops 11–16, pp. 15–19): Homes of Plants (habitat, adaptation), Desert and Mountain Plants, Water and Rain Plants, Coastal Plants (mangroves, erosion), Checkpoint 3, A Library of Plants (herbarium, botanist).
- **Part 4, Caring for plants** (stops 17–18, pp. 20–22): Care and Protection (with a small garden to look after), Checkpoint 4.
- **Grand Review** (stop 19): a 15-question practice test from all four parts, with a Plant Expert certificate that shows which part to revise.

Extras that are not in the book are listed in the game's "For grown-ups" section (for example the pitcher plant's name and the "Do plants sleep?" facts).
