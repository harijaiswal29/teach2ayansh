# The Amazing World of Animals: Jungle Safari Trail

EVS Chapter 5 "The Amazing World of Animals", book pages 22–38. Open [`jungle-safari-trail.html`](jungle-safari-trail.html). Page-by-page notes and all checkpoint answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Ayansh found EVS hard last term, so this game teaches every idea with an everyday example first (a sweater on a hot day, hide-and-seek, kitchen tools for beaks, a hill-station trip for migration), then practises it in exam-style questions: fill in the blanks, true or false with the corrected sentence, match the following, one-word answers, Who am I?, spellings of the key words, and short answers built as full sentences. Teaching is read aloud by default, and the last round of each stop is an "on your own" round with clues only after the second try. Mascot: Chamki the chameleon.

- **Part 1, Homes, body and colour** (stops 1–6, pp. 22–28): Something Looks Wrong! (habitat), The Sweater Puzzle (adaptations and their three types), Body Adaptations, Hide and Seek (camouflage), Copycats in Nature (mimicry), Checkpoint 1.
- **Part 2, Teeth and beaks** (stops 7–9, pp. 29–33): Teeth Tell the Tale (herbivores, carnivores, omnivores, insectivores), Beak Buffet (five beak shapes, Beak Test Challenge, snake Detour, cave painters), Checkpoint 2.
- **Part 3, Clever behaviours** (stops 10–13, pp. 33–35): Migration, Hibernation, Protection Behaviours, Checkpoint 3 (with the Creative Hunt).
- **Part 4, Humans adapt too** (stops 14–17, pp. 35–38): Humans Adapt Too, Roshni's Seasons (retold in our own words), Checkpoint 4, Caring for Animals.
- **Grand Review** (stop 18): a 12-question exam practice paper, three from each part, with a Chapter 5 Animal Expert certificate that shows which part to revisit.

The "My exam word badges" card on the map collects the chapter's key words with their meanings, for quick revision before a test.
