## p24 How Do Animals Live and Survive?
- Animals live in forests, water, hot deserts, icy cold regions. Each animal special.
- Animals eat different food, live comfortably in different places, have different body features.
- These features help animals live well in their **habitats**.
- **A habitat is the natural home of an animal. It is a place where an animal finds food, water, air and shelter.**
- Wonder Junction: woollen sweater on a hot day — how would you feel? Animals cannot change clothes like us. So how do they survive?

## p25 Adaptations
- Special features that help animals survive in their habitat = **adaptations**. Help stay safe, survive in different weathers, live comfortably.
- **A habitat is a place where animals live and adaptations help them live there.**
- Three main types: **Body adaptations, Food adaptations, Behaviour adaptations** (pictures: giraffe, fox eating, bear in cave).
- **Body adaptations** = special physical features (special body parts and colour) that help animals live in their surroundings.
  - Giraffe: long neck → reach leaves on tall trees.
  - Owl: big sharp eyes → see at night.
  - Woodpecker: strong head → pecks wood to find food.

## p27 Trail Treasure: Copycats in Nature — **mimicry**
- Some animals look like other animals to stay safe = mimicry. Scary/dangerous-looking animals are left alone; some harmless animals are born looking similar.
- Viceroy butterfly looks like bitter-tasting Monarch butterfly → predators avoid it.
- Milk snake has stripes like poisonous Coral snake → predators get confused.

## p29 Food Adaptations
- Animals have special teeth and beaks to eat the food they need. Grouped by the kind of food they eat:
- **Herbivores** eat plants; **flat teeth** to chew plants. e.g. cow, elephant.
- **Carnivores** eat meat; **sharp teeth** to tear meat. e.g. lion, tiger.
- **Omnivores** eat both plants and meat; **sharp and flat teeth**. e.g. bear, pig.
- **Insectivores** eat insects; **long tongues** to catch insects. e.g. anteater, frog.
- Birds eat food without teeth.

## p30–31 Birds and their beaks (Wonder Junction: How do birds chew food?)
- Birds have beaks instead of teeth. Beak shape matches the food it eats.
- Grains and seeds → **short, hard beaks** to crack seeds: sparrow, pigeon.
- Flesh → **strong, curved beaks** to tear meat: eagle, vulture.
- Plants and small animals from water → **broad, flat beaks** to filter food from water: duck, swan.
- Nectar → **long, thin beaks** to suck nectar: humming bird, honey eater.
- Fish/small animals from water → **long and sharp beaks** (book says "to filter food from water" — likely a slip; game says "to catch fish from water"): kingfisher, pelican.
- Detour: snakes are not wet and slimy; scales are dry, smooth, may look shiny.

## p33–34 Behavioural Adaptations
- Animals also behave in special ways. **Behavioural adaptations are special ways animals act to survive** — to find food, stay safe or live in different kinds of weather.
- **Migration**: some animals move from one place to another to find food or warm weather. Birds fly to warmer places when it becomes very cold; **the Siberian crane comes to India in winter**. **Whales** swim to warm seas to give birth to their babies.
- **Hibernation**: in very cold weather food is hard to find; some animals sleep for a long time to save energy = hibernation. **Bears** sleep in their den during winter. **Frogs** rest in mud or under leaves in cold weather.
- **Protection behaviours**: special actions to protect themselves from enemies, stay safe without fighting. **Skunk** sprays a bad smell to scare enemies away. **Pufferfish** blows up its body to look big and dangerous.

## p35 Human Adaptations — Did You Know? Humans Adapt Too?
- Just like animals, humans adapt to their surroundings to live and stay safe. Touch ice — cold! Arctic fox: "safe and warm with my fur". Boy without thick fur feels cold.

## p36 Human body + food adaptations
- Body: we **sweat** to cool down when it is hot; our **skin** protects us from strong sunlight; different kinds of **teeth** to bite and chew; **nose and eyelashes** keep dust out.
- Food: people eat food based on where they live and what their body needs. Cold places → **warm, energy-rich food**. Hot places → **juicy food**. We **cook** food to make it soft and safe. We use **tools and fire** to prepare food.

## p37 Human behavioural adaptations
- Things humans do to live safely and comfortably: wear clothes according to the weather; live in houses to stay safe; drink water when thirsty and rest when tired.
- **Animals are born with their adaptations. Humans use their thinking and actions to adapt.** Adaptations help all living things live safely in their homes.

## p38 Eco Route: Caring for Animals
- Animals are suited to certain homes (forests, oceans, deserts, cold places). Human actions change these homes: **cutting trees** removes homes for birds and monkeys; **throwing waste in water** harms fish and turtles; **building roads and cities** reduces animal living spaces. When homes change too much, animals find it hard to survive even with adaptations.
