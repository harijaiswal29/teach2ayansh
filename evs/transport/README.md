# Transport: Ricky's Road Trip

EVS Chapter 9 "Transport: Solving the Moving Puzzle", book pages 45–56. Open [`ricky-road-trip.html`](ricky-road-trip.html). Page-by-page notes are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Ayansh found EVS hard last term, so this game teaches before it asks: every idea starts with a 💡 card and an everyday example, and after each answer the full correct sentence or a model exam answer is shown and read aloud. Teaching is read aloud by default, and the last round of each stop is an "on your own" round with clues only after the second try.

- **Part 1, Moving around** (stops 1–3, pp. 45–47): The Busy Road (what transport is), Transport Troubles (traffic, air pollution, good roads), Think Like a Designer (see, ask, think, try, check).
- **Part 2, Managing traffic** (stops 4–5, pp. 47–48): The Traffic Signal, Round and Round (the roundabout and India's famous circles).
- **Part 3, Clever answers around the world** (stops 6–9, pp. 48–52): Germany's Autobahn, Pedal Power (Eco Route), Colombia's Cable Cars, The Grand Trunk Road.
- **Part 4, Our turn** (stops 10–11, pp. 53–56): India's Moving Puzzles, My Moving Puzzle (his own design to copy into the book).
- **Exam Corner** (stops 12–13): Key Word Wizard (meanings, one-word answers, spelling) and Exam Practice (fill in the blanks, true or false, match the following, short answers). Questions are drawn fresh each time.
- **Grand Review** (stop 14): 10 mixed questions from the whole chapter, with a Moving Puzzle Solver certificate.

The grown-ups section in the game has a quick-revision list of exam questions with answers.
