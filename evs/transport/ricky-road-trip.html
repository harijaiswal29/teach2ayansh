              Quick revision: questions to ask him before the exam

              1. What is transport? It helps people and things move from one place to another (cars, buses, trains, bicycles, boats, aeroplanes).
              2. Name two problems transport creates. Too much traffic; smoke from vehicles causes air pollution (also unsafe, crowded roads).
              3. What do good roads need? Smooth surfaces, clear signs and careful rules.
              4. The five designer steps? See the problem, ask why, think of ideas, try one idea, check if it works.
              5. What is a traffic signal? What do the colours mean? Red, yellow and green lights that control vehicles and pedestrians. Red stop, yellow wait, green go.
              6. Why were traffic signals invented? To prevent accidents at busy intersections, control vehicles and pedestrians, and improve traffic flow.
              7. What is a roundabout? A circular road junction with a small island in the middle; vehicles go round in the same direction. Signals still work better at very busy intersections.
              8. What is the Autobahn? Germany's wide, well-planned highway for fast travel between cities, with no roundabouts or traffic signals.
              9. Why is a bicycle good? It runs on your energy and makes no smoke: cleaner air, quieter roads.
              10. How did Medellin (Colombia) solve its hill problem? Cable cars: small cabins hanging from strong cables carry people up and down the hills.
              11. What is the Grand Trunk Road? One of India's oldest road routes, improved and rebuilt by Sher Shah Suri, with trees, wells and resting places called sarais.
