# Seasons and Lifestyle: Season Quilt Trail

EVS Chapter 8 "Seasons and Lifestyle", book pages 29–44. Open [`season-quilt-trail.html`](season-quilt-trail.html). Page-by-page notes and answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

EVS was hard last term, so every stop teaches first: a 💡 idea card with a real-life example, read aloud by default, before any question. Every stop then ends with an ✏️ Exam corner, done on your own (clues only after the second try), in the formats exams use: fill in the blanks, true or false (with the correct sentence when false), match the following, one-word answers spelt with letter tiles, and answers built as a full sentence. The guide is Ritu the sparrow ("ritu" means season), and finishing a stop sews its patch onto the Season Quilt.

- **Part 1, Nature's clues** (stops 1–3, pp. 29–31): The Season Quilt, Weather or Season?, India's Six Seasons (the ritus wheel).
- **Part 2, One season, many places** (stops 4–5, pp. 32–33): Season Trail Across India, Meet a Climatologist (Dr. Roxy Mathew Koll).
- **Part 3, We change too** (stops 6–9, pp. 33–39): The Fabric Lab, Seasons Café, Festivals Say Thank You, Homes for Every Weather (with a house builder).
- **Part 4, Checkpoints and changing clues** (stops 10–12, pp. 39–44): Read the Quilt (Checkpoint 1), When Nature's Clues Change (Eco Route, Checkpoint 2), The Last Patch (My Quilt of Nature).
- **Part 5, Exam practice** (stops 13–15): Key Words (14 words, word search, spelling), Practice Paper (sections A–E, new questions each time), Grand Review (10 mixed questions, with a Season Quilt Master certificate).

The grown-ups section at the bottom of the map has a revision sheet with every question and answer, for a quick oral quiz before the exam.
