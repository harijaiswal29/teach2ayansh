# Maths Chapter 10 "Time" — page notes (Lighthouse flipbook, pages 58–78)

p58 Chapter opener: boy planning day (wake up 7:00, school, breakfast, play). "What helps us plan our day and know when things happen?"
p59 Intro: time helps organise day; tool = clock. "Clock — What time is it?" (clock at 10 o'clock). Let's Recall: short hand = hour hand, long hand = minute hand. Minute hand on 12 = o'clock; hour hand tells hour. Spotlight: read hour hand first, then minute hand.
p60 10 o'clock = 10:00 digital (two zeros = zero extra minutes). GRIP-1: draw hands for 11:00 and 8:00. Real Route: football practice at 5 o'clock, draw hands. "Half Past" section: Radha's pizza cut in two halves — can a clock be divided into halves?
p61 Discovery Route activity: straw clock. Each small line = 1 minute; number to number = 5 minutes; skip count by 5 as minute hand moves 1,2,...6. Reflect: minutes at 6 = 30; hour hand between two numbers; "Half past __" or __:30. Challenge: more minutes to reach 12 again (30).
p62 Guidepost: minute hand 12→6, hour hand moves halfway to next number. Examples: half past 4 = 4:30; half past 2 = 2:30; half past 10 = 10:30. Short hand exactly between two numbers → half past the previous number. Spotlight: minute hand on 12 = o'clock; on 6 = half past.
p63 GRIP-2: 1) Match 1:30/7:30/11:30 ↔ half past 1/7/11. 2) Train track: draw hands at stops 10:00, 10:30, 11:30, 1:00, 2:30, 4:00 → Sunnyside Station.
p64 Detour "Same clock, two readings": common mistake — long hand on 6 read as "6 o'clock"; correct is half past 11 (11:30), hour hand between 11 and 12. Real Route "My Daily Clock": write time for wake up, school starts, lunch, play time, bedtime. Trail Treasure: 'clock' comes from 'clocca' = bell; old clocks rang bells.
p65 Think Route: Sonu draws "half past 12" with short hand ON 12 and long hand on 6 — is it correct? (NO: short hand should be halfway between 12 and 1). "A.M. and P.M." section. Wonder Junction: breakfast vs dinner both at 7 o'clock — same? 12 hours + 12 hours = 24 hours; every hour 1–12 comes twice. a.m. = midnight to noon (wake up, breakfast, school). p.m. = noon to just before midnight (play in garden, dinner, bed).
p66 Spotlight a.m. = morning, p.m. = afternoon/evening. Guidepost day table: 6:30 a.m. wake up (sun rising), 7:00 a.m. breakfast, 8:00 a.m. school bus, 10:00 a.m. maths class, 3:30 p.m. school ends, 6:00 p.m. cricket, 8:00 p.m. dinner, 9:00 p.m. bedtime (moon out).
p67 GRIP-3: 1) a.m./p.m.: breakfast 7:00 (a.m.), playing in park 5:00 (p.m.), going to sleep 9:00 (p.m.), morning assembly 8:00 (a.m.), watching stars 10:00 (p.m.). 2) Tick most likely: David morning exercises → 7 a.m. (opts 7 a.m., 11 a.m., 3 p.m., 12 noon); Megha cooking dinner → 7 p.m. (7 p.m., 11 a.m., 3 a.m., 1 p.m.); Rahul lunch → 1 p.m. (8 a.m., 1 p.m., 6 p.m., 5 a.m.).
p68 Real Route Think and Share: when do you wake up (a.m./p.m.), event always a.m., event always p.m. "Estimating Duration" section. Wonder Junction: write your name as many times as you can before 1:00 timer stops; count; name a school activity longer than writing your name. Short activities measured in minutes, long activities in hours.
p69 Minutes and Hours. Minute = short, hour = longer. Minutes: brushing teeth ~2 min, washing hands with soap ~1 min, reading one page a few min. Hours: dance class ~1 hr, playing in park ~1 hr, movie ~2-3 hrs.
p70 GRIP-4 minute or hour: playing outside (hour), washing hands before lunch (minute), long train journey across city (hour). Think Route: can two children take different time for same activity?
