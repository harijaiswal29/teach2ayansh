# Subtraction: Take-Away Bazaar

Chapter 8 "Subtraction of 3-digit Numbers", book pages 7–27. Open [`take-away-bazaar.html`](take-away-bazaar.html). Page-by-page notes and answers are in [`book-notes.md`](book-notes.md).

Hands-on tools: place-value blocks you tap to cross out or break (hundreds blue, tens green, ones orange), an H T O column with tap-to-borrow, expanded form, and bar models.

- **Part 1, without regrouping** (stops 1–6, pp. 7–13): Block Shop, Expand & Subtract, Cross Out Blocks, Expand It, Line Them Up, Secret Vault.
- **Part 2, regrouping by breaking** (stops 7–9, pp. 14–17): Break It Up, Regroup Guidepost, Fill the Blanks.
- **Part 3, with regrouping** (stops 10–15, pp. 17–23): Not Enough Ones, Borrow Again, Borrow a Hundred, GRIP-3 Practice, Notebook Store, Riddle Board.
- **Part 4, word problems** (stops 16–17, pp. 24–27): Story Steps, Library & Future Fair.
- **Grand Review** (stop 18): mixed questions from the whole chapter, with a certificate.
