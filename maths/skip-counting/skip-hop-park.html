              My jump badges

              Finish a number line, a number string or a counting game with a jump size, and its badge lights up. Green means forward (add). Orange means backward (take away).

              For grown-ups

              This follows Chapter 4, “Skip Counting”, in Ayansh's maths book (pages 7–23). Each stop uses the book's own examples and numbers, in the same order. A wrong tap costs a little, so three stars means very few slips.

              Part 1 · Forward in 2s, 5s and 10s (pages 7–11)

              1. Number Line Game (pages 7–9): Lisa jumps in 2s, Frank in 5s and Joy in 10s on the 0–20 line. He taps where each child lands, says how much was added each time, and learns the words “skip counting”.
              2. Count in Groups (pages 9–10): the Guidepost balls in 2s (10), grapes in 5s (25) and bundles of sticks in 10s (100). He taps each group to skip count, then picks the total.
              3. Farina & Jacob (page 10): GRIP-1 question 1. Farina starts at 45 and jumps forward in 5s four times (65). Jacob starts at 67 and jumps in 10s three times (97).
              4. Wings & Bulbs (page 11): GRIP-1 questions 2 and 3. Wings on 6 birds (12), fingers on 7 hands (35), and the light-bulb strings: 34, 36, 40, 42 and 57, 62, 72, 82.

              Part 2 · Backward in 2s, 5s and 10s (pages 11–15)

              1. Jump Back Game (pages 11–13): the same three children jump back from 20 (Lisa to 12, Frank to 5, Joy to 0), then the Spotlight: forward makes numbers bigger, backward makes them smaller.
              2. Sara & Azra (page 13): the Guidepost lines (10 to 0 in 2s, 25 to 0 in 5s, 50 to 0 in 10s), then GRIP-2: Sara 44 back in 2s four times (36), Azra 90 back in 5s three times (75).
              3. Bunting Flags (page 14): GRIP-2 question 2, all three flag strings.
              4. Checkpoint 1 (pages 14–15): the colour grid (purple 12, blue 28, yellow 45, green 40), “which is not counted” (20), Rohan's mistake (15 should be 14) and the Skill Trail riddle (20).

              Part 3 · Forward in 3s, 4s and 6s (pages 15–19)

              1. Floor Number Line (pages 15–16): the Wonder Junction tasks on a 0–24 line (3, 6, 9, 12 · 4 to 20 · 6 to 24) and the Think and Tell questions.
              2. Bunches & Bags (pages 17–18): Ria's cherries (24), Ruhi's balloons (32), Shyam's shells (48), then GRIP-3 question 1: apples (18), ice cream scoops (16), bananas (30).
              3. Zahra's Beads (page 19): Zahra 15 forward in 3s seven times (36), Sachin 6 forward in 6s three times (24), and the three bead strings.

              Part 4 · Backward in 3s, 4s and 6s (pages 20–22)

              1. Busy Bee (pages 20–21): the Think Route (which reaches 50 fastest), the bee jumping back in 3s from 20, its questions, and the Guidepost lines from 21, 44 and 72.
              2. Emma's Lily Pads (pages 21–22): GRIP-4. Emma 21 back in 3s five times (6), Emily 90 back in 4s three times (78), and the three lily-pad strings.

              Part 5 · Checkpoint 2 (pages 22–23)

              1. Odd One Out (page 22): 25 should be 26, 27 should be 28, 50 should be 54.
              2. Farm Visit (page 23): “which is not counted” (46 and 22), then the Skill Trail farm: 6 cows have 24 legs, and with 2 more cows, 8 cows have 32 legs.
              3. Grand Review: 10 mixed questions from all five parts, with new numbers each time, and a Chapter 4 Skip Counting Champion certificate that shows which part to revisit.

              Notes and small choices

              • Checkpoint 1 on page 14 says “colour the number that comes while skip counting”. The game reads this as the very next number, which gives one number for each colour.
              • A few blank lines in the book don't match the pictures. Page 11 has 6 birds but 5 blanks and 7 hands but 6 blanks, and the farm on page 23 has 6 cows but 5 blanks. The game counts everything in the picture.
              • The open “Think and Tell” and “Think Route” questions (longest jump, 6s versus 3s, reaching 50 fastest) are turned into simple choices. Ask him to explain his answer out loud.
              • On long number lines, he can swipe the line sideways on a phone.

              Do it for real

              • Page 15, Wonder Junction: draw a 0–24 number line on the floor with chalk or tape and jump it in 3s, 4s and 6s.
              • Count real things in groups: pairs of shoes in 2s, fingers in 5s, coins in 10s.
              • On the stairs at home, skip steps and count in 2s going up, and back in 2s coming down.

              The other chapter games are Tick-Tock Town (Time) and the Multiplication Fair.
